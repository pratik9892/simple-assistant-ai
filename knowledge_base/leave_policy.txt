NIMBUS TECHNOLOGIES - LEAVE POLICY

Purpose
This policy describes the types of leave available to employees and how to apply for them. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

Earned Leave (Privilege Leave)
- Employees get 18 days of earned leave per year.
- Earned leave is credited at 1.5 days per month.
- Up to 30 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year. Anything above 30 days lapses.
- Earned leave for planned holidays must be applied for at least 7 days in advance.
- At the time of exit, unused earned leave is paid out (leave encashment).

Sick Leave
- Employees get 10 days of sick leave per year.
- Sick leave cannot be carried forward and is not paid out at exit.
- A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
- Sick leave can be applied on the same day or after returning to work.

Casual Leave
- Employees get 6 days of casual leave per year for personal work or emergencies.
- A maximum of 3 casual leave days can be taken at a time.
- Casual leave cannot be carried forward.

Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays every year. The holiday list for each office is published in the HR portal in December for the next year.

Maternity Leave
- Female employees get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children.
- For the third child onward, maternity leave is 12 weeks.
- Employees can work from home for up to 3 months after maternity leave if their role allows it.

Paternity Leave
Male employees get 15 working days of paid paternity leave. It must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

Adoption Leave
Employees who adopt a child below 3 months of age get 12 weeks of paid adoption leave.

Bereavement Leave
Employees get 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law).

Marriage Leave
Employees get 5 days of paid leave for their own wedding. This can be used once during employment.

Leave Without Pay (LWP)
If an employee has used all their paid leave, they can apply for leave without pay. LWP longer than 5 days needs approval from the department head.

How to Apply for Leave
1. Log in to the HR portal and open the Leave section.
2. Choose the leave type and dates, and add a short reason.
3. The request is sent to the reporting manager for approval.
4. The manager should approve or reject the request within 2 working days.
Leave taken without approval is treated as leave without pay.
