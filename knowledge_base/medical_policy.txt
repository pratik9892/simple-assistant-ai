NIMBUS TECHNOLOGIES - MEDICAL INSURANCE POLICY

Overview
Nimbus provides group medical insurance to all full-time employees from their first day of work. The policy is provided through HealthFirst Insurance, and claims are handled by the third-party administrator (TPA) MediAssist. The full premium is paid by the company.

Who is Covered
- The employee
- Spouse
- Up to 2 children
- Parents or parents-in-law (optional, see below)

Sum Insured
The sum insured is Rs 5,00,000 per family per year. This is a floater policy, so the total amount can be used by any family member.

Parental Coverage
Employees can add either their parents or their parents-in-law to the policy. Parental coverage has a separate sum insured of Rs 3,00,000 per year. The premium for parental coverage is paid by the employee and is deducted from the salary.

Top-up Plan
Employees can buy an optional top-up plan that adds Rs 10,00,000 of extra cover. The top-up premium is paid by the employee.

What is Covered
- Hospitalization expenses (room, doctor fees, medicines, tests) for stays longer than 24 hours
- Day-care procedures such as cataract surgery and dialysis, even if the stay is shorter than 24 hours
- Pre-hospitalization expenses up to 30 days before admission
- Post-hospitalization expenses up to 60 days after discharge
- Ambulance charges up to Rs 2,000 per hospitalization
- Maternity expenses up to Rs 75,000 for normal delivery and Rs 1,00,000 for C-section
- Newborn baby is covered from day one
- Pre-existing diseases are covered from day one (no waiting period)

Room Rent Limit
Room rent is covered up to 2 percent of the sum insured per day for a normal room (Rs 10,000 per day), and 4 percent for ICU (Rs 20,000 per day).

What is Not Covered
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless needed after an accident
- Dental treatment, unless needed after an accident
- Spectacles, contact lenses and hearing aids
- Treatment outside India
- Self-inflicted injuries

Cashless Claims
1. Go to a network hospital. The list of network hospitals is available on the MediAssist app.
2. Show your e-card at the hospital insurance desk.
3. For planned hospitalization, inform the TPA at least 48 hours in advance.
4. For emergency hospitalization, inform the TPA within 24 hours of admission.
The hospital sends the pre-authorization request, and the TPA approves it.

Reimbursement Claims
If you are treated at a non-network hospital, pay the bill and then claim reimbursement. Submit the claim form, original bills, discharge summary and test reports on the MediAssist app within 30 days of discharge.

Annual Health Check-up
Every employee is entitled to one free health check-up per year at partner diagnostic centres.

Outpatient (OPD) Benefit
Employees can claim up to Rs 10,000 per year for doctor consultations, medicines and diagnostic tests that do not require hospitalization.

Contact
For help with medical claims, contact the MediAssist helpline at 1800-425-9449 or email benefits@nimbustech.com.
