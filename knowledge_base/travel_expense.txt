NIMBUS TECHNOLOGIES - TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Purpose
This policy explains how employees should book business travel and claim reimbursement for work-related expenses. All business travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager before booking.

Travel Booking
- All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal (TravelDesk).
- Domestic travel must be booked at least 7 days in advance.
- International travel must be booked at least 21 days in advance and needs approval from the department head.
- Economy class is used for all domestic flights. Business class is allowed only for international flights longer than 8 hours, and only for employees at Director level or above.
- Trains: AC 2-tier is allowed for overnight journeys.

Hotel Limits (per night)
- Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune): up to Rs 6,000
- Other cities in India: up to Rs 4,000
- International: up to USD 180
Employees can stay with friends or family instead of a hotel and claim Rs 1,000 per night as a stay allowance.

Daily Allowance (Per Diem)
The daily allowance covers meals and small expenses during business travel:
- Domestic travel: Rs 1,500 per day
- International travel: USD 60 per day
Receipts are not required for the daily allowance.

Local Transport
- Cab services such as Uber and Ola are allowed for travel between the airport, hotel and client office.
- Use of a personal car is reimbursed at Rs 12 per km. Use of a personal two-wheeler is reimbursed at Rs 5 per km.

Client Entertainment
Meals with clients are reimbursable up to Rs 2,500 per person. The names of the clients and the business purpose must be mentioned in the expense claim.

Expenses That Are Not Reimbursed
- Alcohol, unless approved for a client event
- Traffic fines and parking penalties
- Personal entertainment such as movies or spa services
- Upgrades to flight or hotel bookings
- Expenses for family members travelling with the employee

How to Submit an Expense Claim
1. Log in to the expense tool (ExpenseNow).
2. Create a new claim and upload clear photos of all receipts. Receipts are mandatory for every expense above Rs 500.
3. Add the trip name and business purpose.
4. Submit the claim within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted after 30 days will be rejected.
5. The reporting manager approves the claim, and Finance verifies it.

Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are paid with the next salary, or within 15 working days of approval, whichever comes first.

Travel Advance
Employees can request an advance of up to 70 percent of the estimated trip cost. The advance must be settled within 15 days after returning from the trip.

Contact
For expense questions, email finance@nimbustech.com. For travel booking help, email traveldesk@nimbustech.com.
